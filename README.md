# LUXORA — Beauty & Wellness Website Concept

A premium, Persian-first website concept for a beauty, aesthetics, and wellness clinic.

LUXORA explores how a specialist service business can present its services, approach, and expertise through a calm editorial visual identity and a clear visitor journey—from discovering services to making a consultation inquiry.

<sub>**Project status:** Concept / prototype. This repository is presented as a portfolio design direction, not as a verified production website or a live clinic.</sub>

## Project at a glance

- **Industry concept:** Beauty, aesthetics, and wellness
- **Website focus:** Service presentation and consultation inquiries
- **Content direction:** Persian-first, right-to-left
- **Visual direction:** Editorial, minimal, warm, premium, and confidence-building
- **Responsive intent:** Desktop, tablet, and mobile experiences

## Intended website experience

The project brief describes a website structure intended to include:

- Brand introduction and clinic philosophy
- Service discovery and featured service storytelling
- Specialist profiles using fictional demonstration content
- Results and gallery presentation
- Consultation inquiry form and contact information
- FAQ and visitor guidance
- Responsive navigation and page layouts
- Basic SEO and accessibility considerations

This list describes the intended scope in the project brief. It is not a claim that every listed interaction has been independently verified as complete.

## Design direction

The concept combines a premium editorial layout with a restrained palette of warm ivory, charcoal, muted champagne, and soft neutral tones. The experience is intended to feel calm and sophisticated rather than like a generic medical or salon template.

Persian typography and right-to-left layout are central to the direction, with English brand elements used selectively.

## Technical foundation

The repository contains a React and TypeScript-oriented frontend toolchain, including Vite and TanStack Start-related dependencies. This section describes the repository's technical foundation; it does not imply a production deployment or a completed integration.

## Demo-content boundaries

- Clinic identity, specialists, testimonials, statistics, and contact details are fictional concept content.
- No real clinic, practitioner, patient, or customer relationship is represented.
